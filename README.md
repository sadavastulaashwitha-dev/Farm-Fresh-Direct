# 🌾 Farm-Fresh-Direct
A mobile-friendly web application that connects farmers directly with customers.

Direct Farmer-to-Customer Marketplace

Farm-Fresh-Direct is a mobile-friendly web application designed to connect farmers directly with 
customers and support the direct sale of agricultural and locally made products.

The platform allows farmers to list products such as vegetables, fruits, dairy products, handicrafts, 
and stitching products, while customers can browse products, add them to a cart, place orders, 
and choose payment options such as Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI.

The project focuses on creating a direct, simple, and mobile-friendly digital platform 
that can be easily accessed by rural users.

# Problem Statement

Farmers often depend on intermediaries or middlemen to sell their products. This can reduce the share earned by farmers and limit their direct interaction with customers.
Farmers also need a simple digital platform where they can list their products without dealing with complicated procedures.

Therefore, there is a need for a platform that provides:
Direct connection between farmers and customers
Simple product listing
Easy digital selling
Mobile-friendly access
Simple and clear user interface

# Proposed Solution

Farm-Fresh-Direct proposes a simple digital marketplace that creates a direct connection between farmers and customers. The platform is designed to help farmers display and sell their products without depending heavily on middlemen.

Farmers can list different types of products such as vegetables, fruits, dairy products, handicrafts, and stitching products. Each product can be displayed with its image, name, price, and description so that customers can easily understand and select the products they need.

To make the platform easier for rural users, a simple and mobile-friendly interface is proposed. A voice/microphone feature can help farmers enter product information more easily, especially when typing may be difficult.

Customers can browse products category-wise, select products, add them to a cart, and place orders. The platform also provides payment options such as Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI.

Overall, the proposed solution aims to provide an accessible and convenient platform for direct farmer-to-customer selling while making the buying process simple for customers.

# How the Platform Works

The basic working flow of the platform is:
# Farmer → Add Product → Customer Browses → Add to Cart → Place Order → Payment
1. Farmer Adds Product
The farmer can add a product with its image, price, and description.
2. Customer Browses
Customers can browse the available products and select the items they want.
3. Cart
Selected products can be added to the shopping cart.
4. Order
The customer confirms the purchase and places the order.
5. Payment
Available payment methods include:
Cash on Delivery (COD)
UPI
